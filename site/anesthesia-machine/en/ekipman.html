Note on use

This atlas is for education. The 3D model represents a generic anaesthesia workstation, not a specific brand. Numerical values are approximate and illustrative; the simulations are simplified models. When making clinical decisions, follow the device's instructions for use and your institution's protocols; this atlas does not replace them.
